Delivery & Returns

Last updated: October 2026

UK Delivery (free)

Everything is made to order in the UK. Most items are made in 1–3 working days, then take 1–3 days in the post. Most orders arrive within a week, and many much sooner. Sweatshirts take a little longer to make (3–5 working days), so please allow 7–10 days for those.

Free delivery is by standard post and isn't tracked.

Express Delivery (+£6.99)

For colour-changing mugs, baby reveal mugs and towels you can choose Express at checkout. Your order is still made to order (1–3 working days), then sent by tracked courier for next-working-day delivery. You'll get the tracking details by email once it's on its way.

Christmas Orders

To have your gifts in time for Christmas, please order by 10 December (14 December for sweatshirts). Delivery times can be longer in the Christmas rush, so the earlier the better.

Where We Deliver

We currently deliver within the UK. Outside the UK? Email hello@onjjem.com and we'll let you know if we can deliver to you.

Returns & Refunds

Because all our products are made to order and personalised with your photo, we are unable to accept returns or offer refunds unless the product arrives damaged or faulty.

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery at hello@onjjem.com with a photo of the damage and we will arrange a free replacement or full refund.

Damaged or Faulty Items

We take great pride in the quality of our products. In the rare event that your item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, please email hello@onjjem.com and we will make it right immediately.
