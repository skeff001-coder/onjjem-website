Questions

What size is it?

A4 landscape (11.7 × 8.3", 29.7 × 21cm), wire-bound with a hanging hole. Each month has your photo on the top page and the 2027 dates on the bottom page.

What if I don't have 13 photos?

Add as many as you like, even just one. We'll repeat them in order so every month has a picture.

Will my photo be cropped?

Each page is landscape, so tall (portrait) photos get trimmed at the top and bottom. The previews above show exactly what will print.

How long does delivery take?

Every calendar is made to order in about 5 working days, then posted free. Most arrive in 7–10 days.

Is there anything you can't print?

Please only use photos you have the right to use. We can't print brand logos, characters from films and TV, or anything offensive.

What if something's wrong?

Email hello@onjjem.com with a photo and we'll put it right with a free replacement or a refund.
