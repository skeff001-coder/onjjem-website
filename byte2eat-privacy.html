Byte 2 Eat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Byte 2 Eat ("the app") is made by Owen Skeffington, a UK sole trader trading as ONJJEM ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Photos of your food

When you scan your fridge or cupboard, the photo you take is sent securely to our server and to a third-party AI service so the ingredients can be recognised and recipes suggested. Photos are used only to provide this feature. We do not sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to identify you.

Information you give us

If you create an account or contact us, we keep the details you provide (such as your email address) so we can run your account and reply to you. Your saved recipes and preferences may be stored so they are there next time you open the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details. We receive confirmation of what you have bought so we can unlock it in the app.

Diagnostics

We may receive anonymous crash reports and basic usage information to fix problems and improve the app.

Sharing

We only share information with the service providers needed to run the app (hosting, AI processing and Apple for purchases). We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Scan photos are kept only as long as needed to create your results. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email hello@onjjem.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
