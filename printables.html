Do I really get a Christmas page too?

Yes! We draw a Halloween page and a Christmas page from your photo at the same time. Both appear to download straight after you pay, and both are emailed to you.

Competition rules

Promoter: ONJJEM (onjjem.com), UK. Each colouring page order includes one free entry: upload a photo of the coloured-in page from your download page or email by 8pm UK time, Monday 2 November 2026. Open to UK and US residents; the person entering must be 18+ (a parent or carer). This is a judged competition, not a prize draw: our judges pick the champion, the best colouring on creativity, colour and effort, and our decision is final. The winner is emailed by Thursday 5 November and receives a surprise prize package worth over £100 at our shop prices, posted free: one 11oz ceramic mug printed with their winning coloured artwork, plus one pin badge, one sticker, one fridge magnet, one postcard, one temporary tattoo and one Christmas greeting card, each printed with a cartoon of the family that we make from the photo used for their colouring page (the same image on all six; no alternative images), plus an ONJJEM gift card worth £50 (UK) or $60 (US), valid until 31 March 2027. No cash alternative. With permission we may share the winning artwork and artist's first name.

Can I cancel?

Each page is personalised and drawn the moment you pay, so it can't be cancelled or exchanged. If your download doesn't come through, email hello@onjjem.com and we'll send it.
