Competition rules

Promoter: ONJJEM (onjjem.com), UK. Each colouring page order includes one free entry: upload a photo of the coloured-in page from your download page or email by 8pm UK time, Monday 2 November 2026. Open to UK and US residents; the person entering must be 18+ (a parent or carer). This is a judged competition, not a prize draw: we pick the best colouring on creativity, colour and effort, and our decision is final. The winner is emailed by Thursday 5 November and receives a prize pack of one 11oz ceramic mug, one pin badge, one sticker, one fridge magnet and one postcard, all printed with the same winning coloured artwork (no alternative images), posted free, plus an ONJJEM gift card worth £20 (UK) or $25 (US), valid until 31 March 2027. No cash alternative. With permission we may share the winning artwork and artist's first name.
