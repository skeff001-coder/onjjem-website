Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

UK orders are printed to order and dispatched within 1–2 working days via Royal Mail Tracked 48, arriving 3–4 working days after dispatch. International orders (USA, Canada, Australia, and most of Europe) take 5–14 working days depending on destination.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Yes. ONJJEM delivers to the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, and most of Europe, with international delivery taking 5–14 working days depending on destination.

What if my order arrives damaged or faulty?

Contact hello@onjjem.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the damage, and ONJJEM will arrange a free replacement or full refund.

Can I return or get a refund on a personalised gift?

Because every ONJJEM product is made to order using your own photo, returns and refunds aren't offered for change of mind — only if an item arrives damaged or faulty.

How does the ChromaCoat Mug work?

The ChromaCoat Mug looks completely plain black in the box. Its heat-sensitive ceramic coating reveals your photo in full colour once a hot drink is poured in, and it's dishwasher safe.

What photo quality do I need to upload?

Higher-resolution photos print more sharply, especially on larger sizes like A1 or A0 canvas. A clear, well-lit photo taken on a modern phone camera is usually sufficient for most ONJJEM products.

What is ONJJEM?

ONJJEM is a UK-based personalised photo gifts company that prints customers' own photos onto canvas prints, mugs, jigsaws, frames, coasters, and other keepsakes, with free UK delivery and international shipping available.
