Questions

How long will my order take?

Everything is made to order. Most items are made in 2–5 working days, then posted with free UK delivery.

How does the cartoon option work?

After you add your photo, we show you a free cartoon version. If you love it, add it for £1.99 and that exact cartoon is printed. If not, we print your original photo.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Our gifts ship free across the UK. Outside the UK? Email hello@onjjem.com and we'll let you know if we can deliver to you.

What if something's wrong with my gift?

Email hello@onjjem.com with a photo and we'll put it right with a replacement or a refund. See delivery info.

Is my photo safe?

Your photo is only used to make your order and is never shared. See our privacy policy.
